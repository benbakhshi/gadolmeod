2024 Company Overview

Gadol Meod Industrial Holdings LLC acquires and operates distribution warehouses, cross-dock facilities, and mission-critical logistics assets in growth corridors throughout the United States. Our disciplined, long-term approach emphasizes conservative leverage, resilient tenancy, and cash flow durability for investors and lending partners.

  • 11.2 million rentable square feet under management across 17 metropolitan markets.
  • Weighted-average occupancy of 97.5% with a diversified roster of credit tenants.
  • Institutional capital partners spanning pension funds, insurance companies, and family offices.
  • Multi-cycle stewardship with a focus on enduring asset value and prudent capital allocation.

Investor Information & Reporting

Investment Principles

  • Acquire infill distribution assets adjacent to major transportation and logistics nodes.
  • Maintain conservative loan-to-value ratios with staggered maturity schedules.
  • Partner with high-credit tenants operating essential supply chain networks.
  • Prioritize long-term NAV growth and disciplined cash flow distribution.

Reporting Calendar

  • Quarterly investor letters released in March, June, September, and December.
  • Annual audit and net asset value statement published each February.
  • Monthly KPI updates delivered through the secure investor portal.
  • Lender reporting packages with DSCR and covenant compliance each quarter.
